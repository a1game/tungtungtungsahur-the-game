# 🎮 Tung Tung Tung Sahur Simulator

**Description:**

Step into the chaotic, heartfelt world of *Tung Tung Tung Sahur Simulator*, a quirky yet touching adventure where you play as **Tung Tung Tung Sahur**—the local Ramadan wake-up hero known for his iconic pre-dawn drumming. But there's a twist: he’s lost his **precious bat**, the very soul of his sahur calls, and he's *devastated*.

Wander through sleepy neighborhoods, rooftops, and alleyways under the moonlight, searching for your beloved bat. Along the way, you'll encounter dozens of other bats—wrong size, weird shape, or just plain cursed—but none of them feel quite right. Still, you’ll have to make do, drumming your heart out with makeshift sticks, fish bones, or glowing neon tubes to wake the people for sahur… all while holding onto hope that your one true bat is still out there.

A mix of stealth, rhythm, and mystery, *Tung Tung Tung Sahur Simulator* is a love letter to community tradition, perseverance, and the soul-deep connection between a drummer and his bat.
